# Yomu to-do list

In order. Done items move to the bottom.

1. **Progress through the whole book.** Percent through the book and time left in the chapter, not just the page within the chapter.
2. **Looked-up words list.** A screen per book with every word you tapped, to review or add several to a deck at once.
3. **E-ink mode.** Pure black and white, no shadows or animations, larger tap areas.
4. **Add books from an Aozora Bunko link.** Paste an aozora.gr.jp link in the app and get an EPUB in the library.
5. **Same reading position on every device.** Store your place online so the phone and an e-ink reader stay in step.
6. **Fully offline dictionary.** Dictionary on the device so lookups work without internet. Biggest job, so last.

## Done

- Read EPUBs vertically or horizontally, with jpdb readings, tap-to-look-up and add-to-deck
- Japanese typesetting based on the W3C's Japanese layout requirements
- Light, dark and match-phone appearance; Mincho or Gothic
- Page-by-page reading with a finger-following slide
- Monochrome design
- Underlines off by default
- jpdb results saved per chapter: instant readings on return, offline reading, background word-status refresh
